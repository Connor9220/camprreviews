TL;DR

  • No defects found. The fix is the same NaiveOffset change that was checked against the crash on the issue, and it moves the existing single-point handling below the segment loop.
  • All 2139 CAM tests pass on the PR, including the two new Thicken tests and Adaptive's testRestMachining (the test that aborted in #34148).
  • One behavior change the description doesn't spell out: rest machining now counts the bottom of every straight plunge as cleared, including plunges that start above the section height. That is physically right. I could not measure it against main here.

What the PR does

  1. libarea/AreaClipper.cpp (CArea::NaiveOffset): the "curve of one point → circle" block moves from before the segment loop to after it, and its condition changes from curve.m_vertices.size() == 1 to cPos.m_vertices.empty(). A curve made only of zero-length segments (a repeated point, or a pure Z move flattened to XY) now becomes a circle instead of reaching cPos.m_vertices.front() on an empty list. That call was undefined behavior, and it aborts with _GLIBCXX_ASSERTIONS when libstdc++ checks list::front (#34148).
  2. App/Area.cpp (ClearedAreaSegmentVisitor::line): a move with no XY travel goes to point() at its lower end instead of becoming a zero-length segment.
  3. Tests: test_thicken_point and test_thicken_zero_length in TestAreaOperations.
